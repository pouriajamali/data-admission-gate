explicit owner assertions, exact byte/purpose binding and five synthetic demo cases.

notices retained. Customer-specific code/data/configuration/deployment stays private.

legal rights/external completeness remain NOT_PROVEN even under acknowledged owner
decisions. No live feed, account, trade, downstream action, legal certification,
general quality engine, performance or production claim. Native-OS CI executes each
actual archive; aggregation reuses those exact tested archives without rebuilding.
